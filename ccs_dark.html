The Thesis · CCS Operating Philosophy

“Cardiovascular trials fail at execution, not science. The cardiologist who wins the pitch needs to stay through database lock. The biostatistician needs to see the protocol concept, not just the analysis. The core lab needs to be reading images, not just storing them.”

After thirty years in this field, we built CCS because no one else was doing it this way.

Marvin Konstam, MD  &  James Udelson, MD — Founders, Cardiovascular Clinical Sciences · Tufts Medical Center
Why CCS Exists · The Execution Gap

Every year, promising cardiac therapies fail. Not because the science was wrong — because the execution was.

02 / Late Biostats

The CCS Operating Model

What changes when cardiovascular work stays in-house. Four principles. No exceptions.

01

Cardiologists on Every Protocol

Konstam, Udelson, and senior cardiology staff engage from protocol concept through database lock — not as advisors, as decision-makers.

02

Biometrics Built In

Data management, medical coding, and SAP and mock-shell development in-house, with statistical programming delivered to support sponsor needs.

03

Adjudication In-House

Blinded independent CEC adjudication performed by CCS cardiologists. Chain of custody from CRF to database stays inside CCS. No subcontractors.

04

Core Lab Reads Images

Echo, hemodynamic, MRI, SPECT, and PET read in-house by CCS. Not stored. Not shipped to a vendor. Read by people CCS employs.
